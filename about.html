THE VISION

More Than Just a Cut.

Yankiii Barber Co. was established to bring a modern, refined barbershop experience to the community. Too many men settle for hurried haircuts with uneven blending or unpredictable wait times.

We created a space where attention to detail reigns supreme. From the moment you sit down, we discuss what works best with your head shape, facial contours, and lifestyle before clippers touch hair.

Whether you need a sharp skin fade for the weekend or an executive taper for work, you get our full focus for the entire duration of your appointment.

OUR FOUR PILLARS

The Principles We Stand By.

Every decision in our shop is guided by four uncompromising values.

Precision

No rushed appointments. Every taper, hairline lineup, and scissor blend receives deliberate, focused attention.

Consistency

We document client preferences and cut specifications so you receive the exact same high-caliber cut every visit.

Respect for Time

Our online booking system guarantees dedicated chair slots. When you book for 10:00 AM, your appointment starts at 10:00 AM.

Hygiene First

Clean capes, sterilized tools, fresh razor blades for every client, and a spotless, sanitized workspace.

THE ENVIRONMENT

A Relaxed, Welcoming Space.

We designed Yankiii Barber Co. to be a retreat from the noise of the day. Modern design accents, comfortable leather chairs, curated playlists, and respectful, friendly conversation.

Located conveniently in Biñan, Laguna, our doors are open seven days a week to accommodate busy professionals, students, and families.
